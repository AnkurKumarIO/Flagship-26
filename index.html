About the Summit

Where Ideas Meet
Execution

Flagship is the annual entrepreneurship summit organized by the Entrepreneurship Cell of VNIT Nagpur. It is a confluence of industry veterans, successful founders, and the next generation of builders.

Through keynote sessions, panel discussions, startup expos, workshops, and high-energy networking events — Flagship ignites the entrepreneurial spirit and fosters a culture of bold innovation in Central India.
